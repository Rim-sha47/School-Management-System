About Us

Welcome to The Providence School

We provide a nurturing environment that empowers students to achieve academic excellence and develop essential life skills.

School Overview

The Providence School is a modern educational institution committed to providing quality education from Playgroup to Matric level. The school focuses on academic excellence, character building, leadership development, and preparing students for future success in a positive learning environment.

Our Vision

To develop confident, responsible, and lifelong learners who contribute positively to society through knowledge, leadership, and strong moral values.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, nurturing, and innovative learning environment where students achieve academic excellence, develop critical thinking skills, and become responsible citizens prepared for future challenges.

Admissions

Join The Providence School

A simple, transparent, and merit-based admission process for all aspiring students.

Admission Procedure

1
Registration

Obtain the prospectus and admission form from the campus office or download it online.

2
Assessment / Interview

Students will undergo a basic entry test (for Class 1 onwards) followed by a brief interview.

3
Document Submission

Submit 2 Passport size photos, B-Form copy, Father's CNIC, and previous School Leaving Certificate.

4
Fee Payment & Enrollment

Pay the admission dues to finalize enrollment and receive the welcome kit.

Important Information

Session Dates

Admissions are open for the upcoming academic session. Classes commence on March 1st.

Age Criteria
  • Play Group: 3+ Years
  • Nursery: 4+ Years
  • Prep: 5+ Years
